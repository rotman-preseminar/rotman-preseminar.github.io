Presentation · 30 min Q&A · 15 min

Before the session everyone

  • We expect all students, presenter and attendees alike, to read the paper carefully in advance. JMCs are exempt but always welcome to attend.
  • The pre-seminar starts at 9:30 a.m. sharp. Please be on time. We will record arrivals more than 2 minutes late.

The presentation presenter

  • Presentations last 30 minutes, followed by 15 minutes of Q&A, and should follow the structure of a conference discussion.
  • We expect slides to be clear, professional, well-structured, and conceptually correct.
  • Summary (up to 10 minutes): research question, authors' answer, and the contribution. Everyone should know what the paper's main takeaway is.
  • Literature: only what is essential to situate the paper, not a full review. It can go at the end if that works better.
  • Strengths and weaknesses (20 minutes): emphasize one or two major points rather than many minor ones; we prefer depth over breadth. We would like to hear your personal opinion: there is no right or wrong.
  • For what makes a good discussion, see for example this guide (SSRN).

At the seminar presenter

  • We ask the presenter to handle the clicker and a bottle of water for the speaker, and to help them set up their slides.

During the session everyone

  • We expect everyone to engage actively in the discussion. Please refrain from using laptops or phones during the presentation.
  • We will keep track of attendance, participation, and the quality of discussions. We will share it with your supervisory committee and the PhD coordinator and will form part of your annual progress review.

Faculty

  • Nico and Filippo coordinate the pre-seminar: room booking, scheduling, paper assignment, and logistics.
  • We will invite faculty working in the speaker's field, so that four to five faculty rotate through each session.
  • Faculty should help students understand the big picture: the question, why it matters, and what the limitations are.
  • Coffee and pastries will be provided.
